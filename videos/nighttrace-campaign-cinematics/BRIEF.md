## Intent

Direct the story of NIGHTTRACE — THE LAST LIGHT as one opening cinematic, nine
first-clear interludes, and one finale. The player must understand that every
relit sector restores a memory of daylight and extends a world-sized Trace. The
Sun-Eater expects that Trace to deliver the Last Star; the Bearer instead closes
it as the circuit that resurrects morning.

The work is modular because it lives between campaign runs. The intro and
finale are full cinematic set pieces. Interludes are shorter, purposeful
chapters that reward a first clear, reveal one new fact, and hand the player
directly to the next sector.

## Customizations

- Three-character voice direction: intimate Last Star, restrained Bearer, spatially oppressive Sun-Eater.
- Code-native subtitles, pause, skip, replay, and safe-area-aware mobile landscape controls.
- Reduced-motion and reduced-flash variants keep every story beat legible.
- Cinematics play only on first campaign clears, then become replayable in the Dawn Archive.
- Existing campaign rewards are never delayed or lost if a cinematic is skipped or interrupted.

## Notes

- Preserve the protagonist's mystery: call him the Bearer; do not give him a personal name.
- Keep the Bearer physically active in every scene. He ignites, carries, fires, reforges, climbs, or closes the revelation.
- Avoid cartoon rings, clean HUD geometry, red line bursts, grids, and flat telegraphs.
- Magic reads as physical material: fractured dawn seams, wet reflections, suspended ash, brass mechanisms, spectral cloth, and irregular light.
- Palette: eclipse black `#03070b`, wet navy `#081722`, shadow plum `#24122f`, antique gold `#c9a75f`, dawn gold `#f5d88a`, restrained cyan `#70dce5`, ivory cloth.
- Display voice: an engraved high-contrast serif already present in NIGHTTRACE. UI/caption voice: narrow uppercase sans with generous tracking.
