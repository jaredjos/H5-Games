# NIGHTTRACE cinematic frame language

## Register

Prestige dark-fantasy title sequence, not a game menu and not a mobile advert.
Every frame is a physical place with depth, weather, and a motivated source of
light. Typography behaves like engraved chapter notation laid over film, never
like a floating website card.

## Frame anatomy

1. A full-bleed painted world plate with slow optical push and a dark
   edge-grade.
2. A midground story object: boss remnant, drowned reflection, broken rail,
   hourglass shard, forge mechanism, or world lens.
3. The Bearer in a readable action silhouette. His chest star and Dawncaster
   are the warm focal light.
4. Fine foreground ash, rain, glass, reed pollen, or solar dust that crosses
   the camera at a different speed.
5. Minimal chapter typography anchored to an edge; captions remain separate
   and accessible.

## Motion grammar

- Camera: slow push, measured lateral drift, or one deliberate gravity change.
- Hero: rises, walks, braces, fires, drags, reforges, or plants. Never idles as
  exposition happens around him.
- Last Star: warm breathing exposure and a restrained harmonic shimmer.
- Sun-Eater: pressure, negative-space expansion, refraction, and low-frequency
  shadow movement.
- Transitions: focus-pull / color-dip as the primary language; one irregular
  light-seam burn for the Observatory reveal and finale.
- No geometric repetition. The Trace is an irregular celestial fracture, not a
  perfect circle.

## Type

- Chapter numeral: 22–28 px Montserrat, 700, tracking 0.34em.
- Chapter title: 72–104 px Georgia, 400, tracking 0.04em.
- Speaker: 18–22 px Montserrat, 700, tracking 0.28em.
- Caption: 30–36 px Georgia, 400, maximum two lines.
- Light-on-dark body copy uses generous line height and warm ivory rather than
  pure white.

## Accessibility

- Captions are on by default and never depend on color alone.
- A reduced-flash pass replaces exposure burns with a soft gold dissolve.
- A reduced-motion pass freezes parallax and uses opacity-only chapter changes.
- Skip, pause, narration, and captions remain at least 44 px on touch devices.
